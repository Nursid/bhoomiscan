# Privacy Policy for TrustLedge

**Effective Date:** August 11, 2026  
**Last Updated:** August 11, 2026  

Welcome to **TrustLedge** ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal data and respecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application **TrustLedge** (the "App").

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not access or use the App.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect information that identifies, relates to, describes, or could reasonably be linked with you ("Personal Data").

### A. Personal & Identity Information
- **Account Details:** Name, Email Address, Phone Number, Profile Picture.
- **KYC & Verification Data:** Government-issued ID details (e.g., Aadhaar Number / Verification data processed via authorized KYC verification providers) for identity authentication and property/land recovery verification.
- **Authentication Data:** Account credentials managed securely through Firebase Authentication and Google Sign-In.

### B. Payment & Financial Information
- **Transaction Details:** Payment status, transaction IDs, and invoice details processed when you pay for services within the App.
- *Note:* All payment processing is securely handled by our third-party payment gateway **Razorpay**. We do **not** store your full credit/debit card numbers or bank PINs on our servers.

### C. Technical & Device Information
- **Device Identifiers:** Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and network information.
- **App Usage Data:** Crash reports, performance logs, and interaction logs to improve app functionality.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:
1. **Identity & KYC Verification:** To verify user identity and prevent fraud during land/property recovery and identity proofing.
2. **Account Provisioning:** To create, manage, and secure your user account via Firebase Authentication and Google Sign-In.
3. **Transaction Processing:** To process service payments and generate digital receipts via Razorpay.
4. **App Improvement & Security:** To monitor app performance, fix bugs, and prevent unauthorized access or malicious activity.
5. **Customer Support & Communication:** To respond to your inquiries, send important notifications, and provide updates regarding your account or requests.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We only share information with trusted third-party service providers necessary to operate our App:

- **Google Play Services & Google Sign-In:** For user authentication and app distribution. ([Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy))
- **Firebase (Google LLC):** For authentication, database storage, and secure cloud operations. ([Firebase Privacy Policy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy))
- **Razorpay:** For secure payment gateway processing. ([Razorpay Privacy Policy](https://razorpay.com/privacy/))
- **Authorized KYC Partners (e.g., Decentro / Government Portals):** For identity verification and Aadhaar/KYC validation services.

---

## 4. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.
- Data transferred between the app and servers is encrypted using **TLS/HTTPS**.
- Account data is stored securely in encrypted databases.

---

## 5. Data Retention & Account / Data Deletion Rights

### Data Retention
We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

### Your Right to Account & Data Deletion
Under Google Play Store policies, you have the right to request deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time.

**How to Request Account & Data Deletion:**
1. Open the **TrustLedge App** -> Go to **Profile / Settings** -> Select **Delete Account**.
2. Alternatively, send an email request to our privacy team at **support@trustledge.com** with the subject line *"Account Deletion Request"* and your registered email address.

Upon receiving your request, we will permanently delete your personal account data within **30 days**, except for data we are legally required to retain for regulatory compliance.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

Our App is **not intended for children under the age of 18**. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from anyone under the age of 18. If we learn that we have collected personal data from a child, we will delete that information immediately.
